Tester agreement

Beta Test Agreement v2, 10 October 2026. This is the full text of what the gate asks you to sign. The gate shows a summary; this page is the agreement itself, and it can be read, saved or printed before signing.

This text is in force as written while it is under review by counsel. If it changes, the date at the top changes with it. Testers who signed v1 are asked to sign this version once; nothing about you leaves your device.

Plain summary (not a substitute for the agreement below)

  • You get a private, unfinished copy of Nobodyssey, Book One, to play and tell us about. It stays ours.
  • Until Book One is released, keep it to yourself: no sharing the link, no streaming, recording, screenshots or posts about what is inside. The one thing you may share is the official trailer on the Book One page, unchanged.
  • Don't copy, download, pull apart or reuse any of it. Play it in your browser, that's all.
  • What you tell us (bugs, ideas, notes) we may use freely, without paying you or naming you.
  • It is a test build: it may break, change or vanish. We give no promises about it.
  • If you break the rules we can go to court to stop it and to recover our losses and legal costs.
  • You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must read this and agree too.
  • New York law applies. Nothing about you leaves your device unless you email us.

This agreement is between AZTMM HLDGS LLC, a limited liability company ("AZTMM", "we", "us"), and the person who signs it at the gate ("you"). If you are under 18, it is also between AZTMM and the parent or legal guardian who consents for you (section 11).

  • "Build" means every pre-release version of Nobodyssey (any Book, chapter or level) that AZTMM makes available to you, and everything in it or delivered with it: code, art, animation, music, sound, text, story, characters, levels, maps, mechanics, systems, user interface, save files and the web pages that serve it.
  • "Confidential Information" means the Build and any non-public information about it or about AZTMM's plans that you see or are told because you are a tester, including the private link, release dates, unannounced features, and the content of later chapters.   • "Feedback" means anything you send or say to AZTMM about the Build or the game: bug reports, notes, ideas, suggestions, recordings or screenshots you send only to us, and survey answers.
  • "Public Release" means the date AZTMM makes a given part of the game generally available to the public without this agreement. Release of Book One does not release later Books.
  • "Personal Use" means playing the Build yourself, for no money, on devices you own or control, through the web browser at the address AZTMM gave you.

3. Confidentiality

3.1 Your promise. Until the relevant part of the game reaches Public Release, you will keep Confidential Information confidential. In particular you will not:

  • share, post or forward the private link, or let anyone else play on your sign-in or your device in your place;
  • stream, broadcast, record, capture, screenshot or photograph the Build, except for screenshots or recordings you send only to AZTMM as Feedback;
  • post, publish or describe the Build's contents in public or in any group chat, forum, server, social network, review, video or article, including spoilers, impressions and reviews;
  • use Confidential Information for anything other than testing the Build.

You may say that you are a Nobodyssey tester, and you may share the Trailer exactly as AZTMM published it.

3.2 What is not confidential. Information is not Confidential Information if you can show that it (a) is or becomes public through no breach by you or anyone you told, including through AZTMM's own announcements, trailers or the Public Release; (b) was already known to you without a duty of confidence before AZTMM gave it to you; (c) comes to you lawfully from someone else who owed no duty of confidence; or (d) you developed independently without using the Build. Homer's Odyssey itself, and anything in it, is never AZTMM's Confidential Information.

3.3 When the law requires it. If a court, government agency or law requires you to disclose Confidential Information, you may do so to the extent required, and you will tell AZTMM first (at the email in section 15) if the law allows, so that AZTMM can seek protection.

3.4 Reporting to authorities. Nothing in this agreement stops you from reporting a possible violation of law to a government agency or from making disclosures protected by law. Under the US Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)), you will not be held liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or to an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

3.5 If something leaks. If you learn that the link or any part of the Build has got out through you or your device, tell AZTMM promptly and help us stop it.

4.2 What you may not do. You will not, and will not help anyone else to:

  • copy, download, save, mirror or archive the Build or any of its files, beyond what your browser does automatically to run it and keep it working offline;
  • decompile, disassemble, reverse-engineer, de-minify, extract or inspect the code, data or assets to get at them, except to the extent a law that cannot be waived allows it despite this limit;

  • use the Build, its assets or its text to train, fine-tune or evaluate any artificial intelligence or machine-learning system.

4.3 The Trailer. You may share, post and embed the Trailer, unedited, with a link to aztmm.com. You may not cut it, remix it, add to it, or present it as your own or as an endorsement by you that AZTMM paid for (see section 7.3).

8.3 Survival. Sections 3 (for any Confidential Information that has not reached Public Release, until it does), 4.2, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 14 and 15 survive the end of this agreement. The release of Book One ends your confidentiality duty for Book One only; it continues for any later Book, chapter, feature or plan you saw that has not been released.
