01 · What this is

A small studio's retelling of the oldest adventure story

Nobodyssey is an adventure game made from Homer's Odyssey, by one person and the AI working beside him. The people are pixel art. The places around them are built in 3D and lit by the hour: sunset on the beach at Troy, torchlight in its streets, the fire in the Cyclops' cave. This page is Book One of four: the first stretch of the voyage home, from the night inside the wooden horse to the morning you sail away from the Cyclops' island.

Private beta means the whole of Book One is here and playable start to finish, by invitation, and we are listening. Keep it to the people we invited. It is free, and will stay free. What you tell us shapes what we fix before Book Two.

The name. Odysseus tells the Cyclops his name is Nobody, and it is the lie that saves him. The game is about the man who had to become nobody to get home.

What it is not. Not a film tie-in, not a copy of any other game. Every sprite, tune and line was made for this, from the Greek text, with the old translations open beside it. There are no purchases, no ads, no accounts, and nothing leaves your device.

Who it is for. Teens and grown-ups who grew up on handheld adventures, or who read the Odyssey once and want to feel it under their thumbs. Rated Teen: fights land, men can die, never in gore.

02 · The story so far

The way home is longer than the war

Ten years at Troy end in the belly of a wooden horse. Thirty men in the dark, and a city hauling you through its own gate. Then Troy at night: keep to the dark side of the streets or fight your way through, all the way to the house of Deiphobus. Then the camp on the beach, the kings' assembly, and the ships turning for home.

Book One follows the landfalls as Homer tells them. At Ismaros the men take the town and stay too long for the wine, and the Cicones come back at dawn with more men than you have. On the Lotus Shore your scouts eat the flower and forget they ever had a home, and you drag them back to the ships crying. Goat Island feeds the crews for once. In the Cyclops' cave a giant eats your men two at a time, and the only way out is a jar of wine, a sharpened stake, his rams, and a name that is not a name.

You play Odysseus. Not a superhero: a captain who counts his men every night, and loses some. Between landfalls you make camp, and the men eat, mend the ship and tell their stories round the fire. The ones you lose stay lost. The game keeps their names in the Roll so you remember them too.

03 · How to play

Learned as you go

  1. Open the game with the Play button. On a phone, turn it sideways; the picture gets bigger and the controls sit under your thumbs. On a desktop, the keyboard works at once, and so does a gamepad.
  2. Press New game. Each thing you can do is shown once, the first time you need it, by whoever is standing next to you. After that it is yours.
  3. Read the card at the top right. It says what you are doing now, and a bronze diamond marks the person or the place. Lost? Press Tab, or tap Where now?, and Odysseus shows you the way.
  4. Fight with your hands. A fight is one man against one man, in real time. Watch his wind-up, raise your shield or tap it as the blow lands, roll away from the low sweeps, then answer with a chain of cuts. Fill Kleos and finish him.
  5. Stop when you like. P or Start pauses. T opens The Tale and your bag. Your place is saved on the device as you go, and Continue on the title picks it up, even after you close the browser.

The controls

StickLeft thumb. Move, any angle. It floats to wherever your thumb lands.
ATalk, look, pick up, use. In a fight, a quick cut. Three in a row make a chain.
XThe next thing in your hands. In a fight, the heavy blow. Hold it to break a shield; down and X lifts a man off his feet.
BIn a fight, the shield. Hold it to block, tap it as the blow lands to throw him open.
YUse what you are holding. In a fight, the roll.
SpecialA fifth button that appears in a fight: the shield charge, the spear lunge, the step behind him. With Kleos full, a god's favour.
KeyboardArrows or WASD move, Shift walks, Z talks and uses, 1 to 5 choose what you hold, Space crouches in Troy. In a fight: Z cut, X heavy, C shield, Space roll, V special. P pause, T The Tale, Tab Where now?
GamepadLeft stick and the four face buttons, the same way. A shoulder button is the special, Start pauses, Back is Where now?

Settings: stick size, left-handed, button opacity, text size, text speed, read aloud, captions, mono sound, less motion, vibration, picture quality, music and sound levels, challenge (Gentle, Standard, Bold). If the game runs slowly, turn picture quality down.

05 · Inside Book One

What you are getting

The lookPixel-art people in small 3D worlds, with real shadows, the time of day, firelight, haze and depth. You walk them freely.
The sourceHomer's Odyssey, mostly Books 8 and 9, from the Greek with Murray and Butler open beside it. Nothing from any film.
Your menSix hundred names on twelve ships. The ones you lose stay lost; the Roll remembers them.
The fightsReal time, one man at a time. Chain your cuts, read the wind-up, block or parry, roll the sweeps and grabs, lift a man and hit him again in the air. Beaten men are knocked down and thrown, never wounded.
KleosGlory, the thing the bards sing. It fills when you fight well, and when it is full it buys one blow from a god.
MetisOdysseus' cunning. You spend it on tricks and the right words, and win it back at camp, telling your own story by the fire.
The campBetween landfalls the crew eats, makes an offering, mends the ship, fishes, plays knucklebones and tells stories. A man who tells you his story trusts you more.
ChoicesAt each landfall Homer's question is yours to answer, and the voyage remembers what you chose.
The godsSeen only by their signs, never as people. Athena watches whether you honour her. Poseidon does not forget his son. The crew mutters when you push them too far.
The economyBronze, cups, wine and cloaks, picked up as you go and spent on offerings and gifts. Nothing is bought with real money, ever.
SoundThe music and the sounds are made on your device as you play, with the wind and the sea under them. Captions are in Settings.
TechOne file, no download, works offline. Phone, tablet or desktop; touch, keyboard or gamepad. It needs a browser from the last few years.
