Tester agreement

Emberhold Tester Agreement v1, 12 November 2026. This is the full text of what the gate asks you to sign. The gate shows a summary; this page is the agreement itself, and it can be read, saved or printed before signing.

This text is in force as written while it is under review by counsel. If it changes, the date at the top changes with it, and testers are asked to sign the new version. It covers Emberhold only. Nothing about you leaves your device except the signature record described in section 12.

Plain summary (not a substitute for the agreement below)

  • You get a private, unfinished copy of Emberhold to play and tell us about. It stays ours.
  • Until Emberhold is released, keep it to yourself: no sharing the link, no streaming, recording, screenshots or posts about what is inside. What is already public on the Emberhold page of aztmm.com is not secret.
  • Don't copy, download, pull apart or reuse any of it. Play it in your browser, that's all.
  • What you tell us (bugs, ideas, notes) we may use freely, without paying you or naming you.
  • It is a test build: it may break, change or vanish. We give no promises about it.
  • If you break the rules we can go to court to stop it and to recover our losses and legal costs.
  • You must be 13 or older. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must read this and agree too.
  • New York law applies. Your signature is sent to us as our record; nothing else about you leaves your device unless you email us.

1. Who this agreement is between

This agreement is between AZTMM HLDGS LLC, a limited liability company ("AZTMM", "we", "us"), and the person who signs it at the gate ("you"). If you are under 18, it is also between AZTMM and the parent or legal guardian who consents for you (section 11).

2. Definitions

  • "Build" means every pre-release version of Emberhold (any chapter or level) that AZTMM makes available to you, and everything in it or delivered with it: code, art, animation, music, sound, text, story, characters, levels, maps, mechanics, systems, user interface, save files and the web pages that serve it.
  • "Confidential Information" means the Build and any non-public information about it or about AZTMM's plans that you see or are told because you are a tester, including the private link, release dates, unannounced features, and the content of later chapters. Section 3.2 lists what is not Confidential Information.
  • "Feedback" means anything you send or say to AZTMM about the Build or the game: bug reports, notes, ideas, suggestions, recordings or screenshots you send only to us, and survey answers.
  • "Public Release" means the date AZTMM makes a given part of the game generally available to the public without this agreement. Release of one chapter does not release later chapters.
  • "Personal Use" means playing the Build yourself, for no money, on devices you own or control, through the web browser at the address AZTMM gave you.

3. Confidentiality

3.1 Your promise. Until the relevant part of the game reaches Public Release, you will keep Confidential Information confidential. In particular you will not:

  • share, post or forward the private link, or let anyone else play on your sign-in or your device in your place;
  • stream, broadcast, record, capture, screenshot or photograph the Build, except for screenshots or recordings you send only to AZTMM as Feedback;
  • post, publish or describe the Build's contents in public or in any group chat, forum, server, social network, review, video or article, including spoilers, impressions and reviews;
  • use Confidential Information for anything other than testing the Build.

You may say that you are an Emberhold tester.

3.2 What is not confidential. Information is not Confidential Information if you can show that it (a) is or becomes public through no breach by you or anyone you told, including through AZTMM's own announcements, the stills, maps, storyboards and text on the Emberhold page of aztmm.com, or the Public Release; (b) was already known to you without a duty of confidence before AZTMM gave it to you; (c) comes to you lawfully from someone else who owed no duty of confidence; or (d) you developed independently without using the Build.

3.3 When the law requires it. If a court, government agency or law requires you to disclose Confidential Information, you may do so to the extent required, and you will tell AZTMM first (at the email in section 15) if the law allows, so that AZTMM can seek protection.

3.4 Reporting to authorities. Nothing in this agreement stops you from reporting a possible violation of law to a government agency or from making disclosures protected by law. Under the US Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)), you will not be held liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or to an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.

3.5 If something leaks. If you learn that the link or any part of the Build has got out through you or your device, tell AZTMM promptly and help us stop it.

4. Your licence to play

4.1 Grant. Subject to this agreement, AZTMM gives you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable, free licence to use the Build for Personal Use for the purpose of testing it and giving Feedback, until this agreement ends.

4.2 What you may not do. You will not, and will not help anyone else to:

  • copy, download, save, mirror or archive the Build or any of its files, beyond what your browser does automatically to run it and keep it working offline;
  • decompile, disassemble, reverse-engineer, de-minify, extract or inspect the code, data or assets to get at them, except to the extent a law that cannot be waived allows it despite this limit;
  • modify, translate, adapt or make anything based on the Build, including mods, fan art based on unreleased content, maps, wikis, guides or datamines;
  • sell, rent, lend, share or publish the Build or any part of it;
  • remove or alter any copyright, trademark or other notice;
  • use cheats, bots or scripts to automate play, except tools AZTMM gives you;
  • try to get around the tester gate, or access parts of the Build or the site that were not offered to you;
  • use the Build, its assets or its text to train, fine-tune or evaluate any artificial intelligence or machine-learning system.

You waive, and agree not to assert against AZTMM, its licensees or its successors, any claim (including any claim for payment, credit, attribution or under moral rights, to the extent you may lawfully waive it) that arises from AZTMM's use of Feedback, or that any part of the game, now or later, was taken from or is similar to your Feedback or to any idea you share. 7.1 Use the Build lawfully and do not use it to harass anyone.

7.2 Keep any device you use to play reasonably secure, so others cannot reach the Build through it.

7.3 Posts and endorsements. If AZTMM ever invites you to post about the game, or gives you anything of value (early access, credits, merchandise, a free copy of a paid product), any post you make must clearly disclose that connection (for example, "I'm an Emberhold tester") as the US Federal Trade Commission's endorsement guidance requires. AZTMM will never ask you for a positive review, and will never condition anything on one.

8. Term, ending and survival

8.1 Term. This agreement starts when you sign it at the gate and lasts until the earlier of (a) Public Release of Emberhold, and (b) the date either of us ends it. You may end it at any time by stopping play and clearing the site's data in your browser. AZTMM may end it, or your access, at any time for any reason, including by withdrawing the Build or changing the link.

8.2 On ending. Your licence ends. Stop using the Build and clear the site's data from every browser where you played (the Privacy page explains how).

8.3 Survival. Sections 3 (for any Confidential Information that has not reached Public Release, until it does), 4.2, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 14 and 15 survive the end of this agreement. The release of Emberhold ends your confidentiality duty for what was released only; it continues for any later chapter, feature or plan you saw that has not been released.

9.3 Legal fees. In any action to enforce this agreement, the prevailing party may recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the other.

11. Age and parents' consent

11.1 You must be 13 or older to be a tester. The game is made for players 13 and up.

11.2 If you are 13 to 17, a parent or legal guardian must read this agreement and consent at the gate by entering their own full name. By doing so the parent or guardian (a) agrees to this agreement on the tester's behalf and on their own behalf, (b) agrees to supervise the tester's compliance, and (c) agrees to be responsible for any breach by the tester, as if they had committed it.

11.3 If AZTMM learns that a tester is under 13, or under 18 without a parent's consent, AZTMM may end that tester's access at once.

12. Privacy

The Build runs in your browser and sends nothing about your play to AZTMM: no account, no analytics, no tracking. Your saved game and your settings are stored only in your browser's local storage on that device. Your signature record (the name or names you type, the time, your choice of age bracket, the version you signed, a receipt code and a short description of your browser) is kept on that device and is also sent once to AZTMM, together with the internet address and country it came from, as AZTMM's record that you agreed; AZTMM keeps it for as long as this agreement can matter and uses it for nothing else. The game's Privacy Policy (aztmm.com/privacy/) describes this in full. If you email us, we keep that email to act on it, and we do not share it.

You agree that typing your full name, ticking the box and pressing Sign and continue is your electronic signature, has the same effect as signing a paper copy by hand, and that this agreement may be formed electronically, under the US Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001 et seq.) and New York's Electronic Signatures and Records Act (N.Y. You agree that the full agreement was shown to you, or linked from the gate where it says "Read the full agreement", before you signed; that you had the chance to read it, save it or print it; and that the version you signed is the one named at the bottom of the gate.

You agree that the receipt, AZTMM's signature record and its invitation record are evidence of your agreement.
