What you are testing

The Warm Court

The first chapter of Emberhold, from before light to the wall top. You play Robin, fifteen, who rakes the ash in a castle kitchen and says good morning every day to something asleep under the yard.

It is one castle yard, ten beats and about ten minutes, in real 3D, on a PC or an upright phone. It is unfinished, and the build changes between visits, so a fresh look is always worth it.

How to play

Learned as you go

On a computer

  • WASD move, and the mouse looks.
  • Z Space Enter E are context or attack. Hold for the sweep.
  • X rolls. C lets the Heat out.
  • Hold Shift or V to guard. Esc or M opens the menu.

On a phone or a gamepad

  • Hold the phone upright. Your left thumb moves, your right thumb looks.
  • A gamepad works too: the left stick moves, A is context or attack, B is the dodge roll.
  • Use a recent browser with WebGL. The first load is a large file, so Wi-Fi is kinder.

What we ask of you

Play it once, then tell us

  1. Sign the agreement the first time you press play. It takes a minute. The full text is on its own page if you want it first.
  2. Play the chapter through, and stop whenever you like. Where you stopped is useful to us.
  3. Write to game@aztmm.com with what annoyed you, what you could not work out, and what broke. Say which phone or computer and which browser you used.

Keep it to yourself. Until Emberhold is released, please don’t share the link, or stream, record, screenshot or post what is inside. You may say you are a tester. Screenshots sent only to us are welcome.
