Nobodyssey

Troy has fallen. Twelve ships and six hundred men turn for home, and you are the one who has to bring them there. Retold from Homer's Odyssey, with pixel-art people in lit 3D places and fights you play with your own hands.

Book One

The wooden horse, and Troy at night, crossed in the dark or by the sword. A feast at Ismaros that goes on too long. A shore where nobody wants to leave. An island full of goats. A cave, a giant, and a name that saves your life. Free to play in your browser on phone, tablet and computer, and it keeps working once it has loaded, even with no signal.
