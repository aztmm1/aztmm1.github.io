Lea

Lea, an astronaut, runs on. The land climbs into ridges and drops into canyons, and every world ends in a wormhole to the next.

Swipe to change lanes, jump and slide. Hold to fly. Twelve worlds, from the Moon to the edge of a black hole and past it, with a flight through a real galaxy between each of them.

Seven boosters drift to Lea in passing, from nitro to anti-gravity, and every world has its storms.

The studio

AZTMM Gaming Labs is a small studio inside AZTMM HLDGS LLC. We write our own stories and make them into games and films you can open anywhere.

When the story is an old one, we go to the source first: Homer for Nobodyssey, the Sabhasad chronicle and the histories of the period for Rajmudra.

Every game here runs in a browser, on a phone held upright or at a desk, with nothing to install.
