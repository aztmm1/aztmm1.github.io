The Lab

AZTMM Gaming Labs is a small studio inside AZTMM HLDGS LLC. We make games and short films that open in a browser, on a phone or at a desk. Each one starts as a story we want to tell, and grows a world, a cast and a look of its own before anyone can play it.

What we make

Story games

Long stories you play through: Odysseus taking twelve ships home from Troy, and the real forts and passes of the Sahyadri.

NobodysseyRajmudra

Small games and puzzles

Games you learn in a minute: a card battle across one river that is over inside three minutes, a run across twelve worlds in space, a table of marked stones to sort at dusk.

EpochLeaSetstones

Films

Short pieces to watch rather than play. The first is a year in pixel art, one small scene at a time, with a black cat somewhere in every frame.

Purrfect Year

What we hold to

  1. Our own stories

    Every story, character and line in these projects is written for that project. None of them borrows the names, look or sound of another game or film.

  2. The old sources come first

    When a project retells something old, we read the old thing first. Nobodyssey follows Homer's Odyssey. Rajmudra is being built from the Sabhasad chronicle and the histories of the period, and nothing goes into it that those sources do not support.

  3. It plays where you are

    Our games and films open in a browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer. There is nothing to install and there are no ads.
