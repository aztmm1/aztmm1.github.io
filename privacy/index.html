Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026. This covers aztmm.com and the games on it, as they are today. The short version: Nobodyssey collects nothing about you. There is no account, no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and nothing you do in that game is sent to us. The puzzle games and Lea count plays without knowing who is playing: no name, no cookie, nothing that points to you. See Play counts below. Lea sends your stars and words about a run only if you choose to give them. The one time we ask for anything is the invite box, which keeps the email address you type so we can send the invite.

The short version: Nobodyssey collects nothing about you. There is no account, no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and nothing you do in that game is sent to us. The puzzle games and Lea count plays without knowing who is playing: no name, no cookie, nothing that points to you. See Play counts below. Lea sends your stars and words about a run only if you choose to give them. The one time we ask for anything is the invite box, which keeps the email address you type so we can send the invite.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your saved progress in your browser's storage on this device, so you can close it and carry on later. Your settings, such as text size, sound and read-aloud, are kept the same way. It also remembers your answer to "Who is playing?" (it only sets text size, reading speed and read-aloud) and which tips you have already seen. So the game can open with no signal, your browser also keeps a copy of the game's files.

If you are a tester, the tester agreement you signed is kept here too: the name you typed (and a parent's, if one signed), the time, your age bracket, a receipt code and a short description of your browser.

None of this leaves your device unless you move your save yourself (see Sharing below), except the tester signature, which is sent to us once (see Testers below). We can't see your saves, and we couldn't get them back for you if they were lost.

Lea and the puzzle games keep their saves and settings in the same way. And for half an hour after another site sends you to aztmm.com, your browser remembers that site's name here, so that Lea can say which site a visit came from (see Play counts below). The music bar at the bottom of the site remembers, only until you close the tab, whether you paused or closed it and which track it had reached. None of that is sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or email address.
  • No cookies.
  • No tracking pixels, fingerprinting or outside analytics services. Nobodyssey sends us nothing. The puzzle games send anonymous play counts, described under Play counts.
  • No ads, and nothing to buy.
  • No chat or messages between players.
  • We never sell or share anything about you.
  • Nothing loads from anyone else's servers. The fonts and pictures come from this site.

Sharing

The "Share the link" button and the game's voyage card use your device's own share menu. Where there is none, they copy the link or save the picture instead. You pick who gets them. We don't see what you share or with whom.

Move my voyage, in Settings, turns your saved game into a link, a code or a small file, so you can carry it to another device. It carries your whole save, though nothing about you. Send it only to yourself or your family. In the link, the save sits after the # sign, and browsers never send that part to a website, so it does not reach us.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud is off until you turn it on, in Settings or by choosing "Me, with someone reading" at the start. It uses the voices already on your device. Only if a device has none does the browser use an online voice, and then the line being read goes to the browser's maker, such as Google or Microsoft, to be spoken. That is the game's own words, never anything about you.

The web host

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees your device's internet address while it sends you the pages, and keeps it in its own logs to protect its servers. We can't see who visits.

Play counts in the puzzle games and Lea

When you play a puzzle game on aztmm.com (Setstones, Dyeworks and Waylight today, and others as they open), the game sends us counts of play so we can tune the levels: which level was started, solved or left, how many moves, undos, restarts and hints it took, how long it took, the awards earned, the game's version, and whether the device is a phone, a tablet or a desk. With them go a random label made afresh every time the page opens, today's day number, and the day number of your first play, which the game keeps in its own save so we can tell how many players come back.

Lea, the runner, sends counts of the same kind: that the game opened and how many seconds it took to load, when a run starts, the farthest world reached, and for each run its distance, score, time, the boosts used and what ended it. With the first of these goes the name of the site that linked you to aztmm.com, for example instagram.com. It is the site's name only, never the page you were on.

There is no name, no account, no cookie and no advertising identifier, and nothing that follows you from one visit to the next or to any other site. The counts go to a small service of ours on Cloudflare, which adds the country the request came from. Our service does not keep your internet address. We use the counts only to make the games better, and we never sell or share them.

You can turn the counts off in the game's menu. Copies of a game played anywhere other than aztmm.com never send them, and games made for young children send nothing at all.

Stars and words after a run

When a run in Lea ends, you can tap one to five stars and, if you wish, add a few words, up to 500 characters. Nothing is sent unless you tap. What you send goes to the same small service, with the run it was about (the world, the distance, the time and what ended it), the game's version, the kind of device and the country. It holds no name and no address, so please don't write anything personal in it. We read it only to make the game better.

The three best scores

When a run in Lea ends, the game asks the same small service for the three best scores made in this version of the game, and shows them under your own three. It sends the game's name and its version number, nothing else, and gets three numbers back. No name stands beside a score, and nothing about you or your device is sent or kept for it.

Testers

When you sign the tester agreement, your signature (the names you typed, the time, your age bracket, the version, a receipt code and a short description of your browser) is sent once to a small service of ours on Cloudflare and stored there with the internet address and country it came from. It is our record that you agreed, used for nothing else, never shared or linked to your play, and deleted three years after Book One is released unless a legal claim needs it. No signal when you sign? It is sent the next time you open the game.

Testers of our other story adventure

Signing its tester agreement makes a record of five things: the name you typed (and a parent's, if one signed), your age bracket, the time, the agreement's version and a receipt number beginning EMB. It is kept on your device and sent once to the same small service of ours on Cloudflare, with the internet address and country it came from. It is used only to show that you agreed. To have it removed, write to game@aztmm.com with your receipt number and we will delete it, unless a legal claim needs it. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the copy on your device.

If you ask for an invitation

The invite box on this site asks for one thing: your email address. When you press the button, the address goes to a small service of ours on Cloudflare and is stored with the game you asked about, the time, and the country the request came from. Our service does not keep your internet address. We use the address only to send you that invitation and never put it on a mailing list. To have it removed, write to game@aztmm.com.

If you write to us

Write to game@aztmm.com. We see your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to answer you and act on what you said. We keep emails for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then delete them, and we never put you on a mailing list.

Deleting your saves

Your saves are yours. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes the saves, the settings, any tester record and the offline copy together.

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings › Apps › Safari › Advanced › Website Data (on older iOS, Settings › Safari › Advanced › Website Data). Find aztmm.com and delete it. If you added the game to your Home Screen, delete that icon too.
  • Chrome, on Android or a computer: open aztmm.com, tap the icon to the left of the address, open the site settings and choose to delete its data.
  • Safari on a Mac: Safari › Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data, then remove aztmm.com.
  • Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data › Manage Data, then remove aztmm.com.

Age

Nobodyssey is made for players 13 and up. Rangmala, the colouring book, is made for young children with a grown-up nearby: it asks for nothing, sends nothing, keeps what a child makes on the device only, and has no links out. No game here asks anyone for an email address or a birthday, and none collects anything about a child, so there is nothing about any child for us to keep. If a child under 13 writes to us, we reply once, ask for a parent, and delete the email.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you (at most, your emails to us), and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to game@aztmm.com and we will answer within 30 days. In the EU and UK we handle your emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you, and you may complain to your data protection authority. In California and elsewhere in the US: we don't sell or share personal information, or use it for advertising.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page will change first, with a new date at the top. If the game ever starts sending anything about you anywhere, we will say so here and in the game before it happens, and ask you first where the law requires.

When you write to game@aztmm.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write or attach (for example, a tester's receipt code, or a screenshot sent to us as feedback). 5. Your rights

Everyone

You may ask us at game@aztmm.com what personal information we hold about you (which, at most, is your emails to us), to correct it, or to delete it. We will answer within 30 days, free of charge, and may ask you to write from the same address to confirm it's you.

EU and UK (GDPR and UK GDPR)

  • Controller: AZTMM HLDGS LLC is the controller only for emails you send us and for testers' signature records. For the data in section 1, which stays on your device and is never sent to us, we do not process personal data at all.
  • Legal basis: we handle your emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the Game (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)); we keep testers' signature records because they are necessary for the tester agreement itself (art. 6(1)(b)) and to establish and defend legal claims. Cloudflare is our processor for the signature records.
  • Device storage: the storage in section 1 is strictly necessary to give you the service you asked for (to save your game and settings), so it does not need your consent under the ePrivacy Directive art. 5(3) or the UK's PECR reg. 6.
  • Your rights: access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability (GDPR arts. 15-21). You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
  • International transfer: we are in the United States, so your email is held in the US.
  • Representative: our processing is occasional and low-risk, so we have not appointed an EU or UK representative (GDPR art. 27(2)(a)).
  • No automated decisions or profiling.

California (CCPA as amended by the CPRA) and other US states

We do not meet the thresholds that make a company a "business" under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the same is true of the other US state privacy laws. Even so: we do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, we collect no sensitive personal information, and we would honour a Global Privacy Control signal if we ever had something to opt you out of. In the last 12 months the only categories of personal information we have received are identifiers and the content of messages (your email address and what you wrote, if you emailed us) and, for testers, identifiers (the signature record above), from you, to answer you or to record your agreement, disclosed to no one but our email host and Cloudflare acting for us.

6. Security

Because we hold so little, there is little to lose. Our email account is protected with a strong password and two-factor authentication. If we ever discover that emails we hold were accessed without permission, we will tell the people affected and any authority as the law requires (including New York's SHIELD Act where it applies). Report a security concern to game@aztmm.com.

7. Changes

If any of this changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top. If a change means the Game would start sending anything about you anywhere, we will say so on the Site and in the Game before it happens, and ask for your consent where the law requires.
