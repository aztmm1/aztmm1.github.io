Overview

Purrfect Year is not a game you win. You watch, and step through the scenes with the arrow keys or the buttons on the screen. Space pauses and F goes fullscreen.

Every picture is drawn in code at 480 by 270 pixels and lit as a whole: windows, lamps, fires and fireworks throw light, and the sky, the stars and the moon change with the scene. Each scene loops cleanly every four minutes.

It is a small, finished piece for a quiet few minutes on a phone or at a desk.

The making

Where the idea came from

Purrfect Year is a diary of one year, from one autumn to the next, kept as sixteen pixel-art scenes with the same black cat in each. Its title card reads 2025 to 26.

It came before the games. The way it draws light, weather, sky and smoke was later carried into Nobodyssey.

Built with
Plain JavaScript drawing pixel art on a canvas of 480 by 270 pixels. Every scene is worked out from the time alone, so it can be stepped through or left to run. It ships as a single page. No engine.
Scenes
Sixteen scenes in the order of a year, each a place and a time of day.

Story

A year drawn as sixteen scenes, one after another. They begin on an autumn night by a river and end, a year on, on another wet autumn night.

The cat is a black one, and he is in every scene. Sometimes he sits at a window with a second cat, sometimes alone on a balcony, and in some scenes a kitten comes and goes. The people round him are small animals who gather in the plaza or walk past.

Ten scenes are the same river block seen in different weather: autumn rain, winter snow, spring blossom, a summer dusk. Four move the cat somewhere new, a big city street at night, a sunny street with palms, a harbour and a house with a garden. The last two are a brick block by the water in autumn.
